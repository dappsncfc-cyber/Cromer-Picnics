Privacy Policy

The White Horse • Cromer Picnics Data Protection

At The White Horse (24 West Street, Cromer, NR27 9DX), we are committed to protecting your privacy and complying with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and Data Protection Act 2018.

1. Information We Collect

When you place an order for a coastal picnic hamper or register an account, we collect:

  • Your full name
  • Contact email address and phone number
  • Collection date, time, and dietary requirements
  • Cool box deposit tracking records (£20.00 deposit per box)

2. How We Use Your Personal Data

Your data is used solely to fulfill picnic orders, manage insulated cool box deposit refunds, send live collection updates, and maintain order history records.

3. Data Storage & Security

All personal data is processed securely via Google Cloud / Firebase Firestore under strict access controls. We do not sell or share customer personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Your Rights under UK GDPR

You have the right to request access to, rectification of, or erasure of your personal data held by us. To exercise your rights or make an inquiry, contact us in person at 24 West Street, Cromer or via your registered order email.
